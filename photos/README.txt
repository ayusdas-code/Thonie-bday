PHOTO GALLERY
==============

Paste your girlfriend's photos in this folder.

Name them like this (JPG recommended):

  1.jpg
  2.jpg
  3.jpg
  ...
  12.jpg

Tips
- Square or portrait photos look loveliest in the polaroid frames.
- You can add more than 12: open js/config.js and raise "photoCount".
- PNG also works if you change "photoExt" in js/config.js to "png".
- Missing numbers will show a cute placeholder until you add that file.
